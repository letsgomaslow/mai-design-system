Standardizing on the Ascent design language

TOAll client-facing teams
FROMDesign
REDecks, documents and social now start from templates

Effective today, every recurring asset starts from a system template. Do not restyle from memory: fork the template, replace the content, keep the system.

Decks use the six masters (title, section, content, stats, quote, closing). Documents use the proposal, memo, or invoice shells. Social uses the OG, square, and banner canvases. Navy carries authority; accents point; pink appears once — on the ask.

Questions and exceptions go to the design channel. If it is not in the system, it does not ship.
